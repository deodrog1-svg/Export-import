# Sulphur / сера / 황 (HS 2503.00)

- Classification: HS 2503.00 = sulphur of all kinds except sublimed, precipitated, colloidal (those are 2802.00). Lump, granular, liquid technical sulphur all go in 2503. CAS 7704-34-9. verified: 2026-10-30
- Korea duty: aggregator lists basic rate 2%, WTO bound 0%, 0% under most FTAs. Not confirmed on UNI-PASS. Kazakhstan/Turkmenistan/Russia have no FTA with Korea. verified: 2026-10-30, source: https://treayo.com/en/hs/korea/25030000
- Korea VAT 10% on CIF + duty (recoverable for registered business).
- Korea hazardous-material law (위험물안전관리법): sulphur (유황) = Class 2 flammable solid, designated quantity 100 kg. Storing more needs a licensed hazmat store; deliver port -> client direct. verified: 2026-10-30, source: https://www.kfsi.or.kr/contents/webzine/201709/sub03-04.html
- Korea K-REACH: importers of >=1 t/yr must register or be covered by the foreign maker's Only Representative (OR). Sulphur-specific status NOT confirmed. Check the K-REACH portal / KCMA before the first shipment. verified: 2026-10-30
- Shipping: formed (granular/prilled) sulphur is generally not regulated as dangerous goods in containers (IMDG SP 242); lump/powder may be UN1350 class 4.1. Confirm with forwarder.
- Korea is a NET EXPORTER of sulphur (refinery by-product). OEC 2023: exports ~$7M, imports ~$0.15M. Korean clients can buy from domestic refiners, so CIS sulphur must beat their price. source: https://oec.world/profile/bilateral-product/sulfur/reporter/kor
