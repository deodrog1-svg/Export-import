# Trusted sources

Prefer official sources. Add country-specific ones as they are found.

## Global
- HS nomenclature: World Customs Organization, https://www.wcoomd.org
- Tariffs (MFN + preferential), rules of origin: ITC Market Access Map, https://www.macmap.org
- Tariff & trade data: WTO Tariff & Trade Data, https://ttd.wto.org
- Product requirements notifications: WTO ePing, https://epingalert.org
- Trade statistics: UN Comtrade, https://comtradeplus.un.org ; ITC Trade Map, https://www.trademap.org
- Regulations per country/product: ITC Global Trade Helpdesk, https://globaltradehelpdesk.org
- Incoterms: International Chamber of Commerce, https://iccwbo.org

## Sanctions & export controls
- UN Security Council consolidated list, https://main.un.org/securitycouncil/en/content/un-sc-consolidated-list
- US OFAC, https://ofac.treasury.gov ; US BIS export controls, https://www.bis.gov
- EU sanctions map, https://www.sanctionsmap.eu
- UK OFSI, https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/office-of-financial-sanctions-implementation

## Major tariffs
- EU: TARIC, https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/dds2/taric ; Access2Markets, https://trade.ec.europa.eu/access-to-markets
- US: Harmonized Tariff Schedule, https://hts.usitc.gov ; CBP, https://www.cbp.gov
- UK: Trade Tariff, https://www.trade-tariff.service.gov.uk

## Freight indices
- Drewry World Container Index, Freightos Baltic Index (FBX), Xeneta
