# Uzbekistan (UZ)

- Sulphur: Uzbekneftegaz sold 436-440 kt sulphur products in 2022; Mubarek GPP exported 38.5 kt in 2022; Mubarek granulated sulphur unit capacity 150 kt/yr. sources: https://yuz.uz/en/news/v-2022-godu-realizovano-4402-tsyachi-tonn-sernoy-produktsii , https://caspianpost.com/uzbekistan/uzbekistan-expands-granulated-sulphur-production-in-mubarek
- No 2026 sulphur export ban found (searched 2026-10-30), but not confirmed with an official source. VERIFY with Uzbekneftegaz / customs before contracting.
- Sales go through the commodity exchange (UzRTSB/UzEx) or Uzbekneftegaz; export contracts must be registered in the foreign trade e-system (Res. 283, 2020). source: https://gratanet.com/news/foreign-trade-operations-is-approved-in-uzbekistan
- Route to Korea: rail -> Kazakhstan transit (Saryagash) -> Altynkol/Khorgos -> China -> Lianyungang -> Busan. Est. ~$3,520 per 20ft, ~30 days. Cheapest open CIS route in our model.
- No FTA with Korea.
