# South Korea (KR)

- Customs: Korea Customs Service, UNI-PASS (https://unipass.customs.go.kr). Duty basis: CIF.
- VAT 10% on CIF + duty, recoverable.
- Importer setup: business registration (사업자등록, Hometax / tax office) -> customs code (통관고유부호, UNI-PASS) -> trade business number (무역업고유번호, KITA, free) for D-9.
- Foreigners who can register a business: F-2, F-4, F-5, F-6, D-8, D-9 (F-4 has some industry limits). D-2, D-10, E-9, H-2 generally cannot. Unofficial sources; confirm on HiKorea. verified: 2026-10-30, source: https://kowork.kr/en/visa/D-9
- No FTA with KZ, TM, RU.
- Chemicals: K-REACH + Chemical Substances Control Act (화학물질 확인 per import via KCMA).
