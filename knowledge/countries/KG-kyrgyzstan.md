# Kyrgyzstan (KG)

- Sulphur: NO meaningful domestic production found. Imports come from Kazakhstan (2025: ~$4.6k, per Trading Economics/Comtrade). Aug 2026 imports from KZ up 5.8x (AKIpress, paywalled). verified: 2026-10-30, sources: https://tradingeconomics.com/kyrgyzstan/imports/kazakhstan/sulfur-all-kinds , https://akipress.com/news:926519
- Any sulphur a Kyrgyz trader sells is Kazakh (or Russian) origin. Re-exporting it to a third country while the KZ ban (27 Jun 2026) is in force = circumvention risk; the certificate of origin would still say Kazakhstan. NOT recommended.
- Route to Korea: truck Bishkek -> Torugart/Irkeshtam -> Kashgar -> China rail ~4,500 km -> Lianyungang -> Busan. Est. ~$5,200 per 20ft, ~38 days. Most expensive CIS option. China-Kyrgyzstan-Uzbekistan railway not yet operating.
- EAEU member. No FTA with Korea.
